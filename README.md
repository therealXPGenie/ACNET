# ACNET
Audio Communication Network
Turn your sound card into a network adapter. An acoustic internet built for off-grid telecommunication.

Acnet is an in-browser system that uses 4-FSK/2-FSK, Gibberlink(ggwave), high-speed video (SSTV) and high resolution (picture) (PD290) over an audio channel. The first of its kind—an inexpensive and simplified HTTPS-over-sound solution. Envisioned to help during internet blackouts, it works like a tactical communication network. Plug and play analog packet radio. Just open ACNET.html with any device that supports Edge Browser. Tested on windows 10 and windows 11. (Tested on a Xbox one S and it is working).
Designed to work with terrible equipment and high end equipment with a reasonable achievable max speed of 200 Bauds on 4fsk.
You have a friend somewhere that you can phone and he really needs internet? You have a loved one and you want to see each other? Now it's possible!
