📡 ACNET

Audio Communication Network — an acoustic internet data-over-sound modem built by AI and XP Genie that turns any device with sound-card into a acoustic network.

No Wi-Fi, no cellular, no cables, no problems. ACNET transmits telemetry data as audio tones between devices (two-way radios/telephones/tranceivers or telecom). Chat with your firends. One machine runs as a relay (with internet access), the others as clients (requesting pages). The relay fetches web content and sends it back over sound. Alternatively you can use Acnet as a browser (works best on Edge browser). Just type your Url in that format in the Url bar "https://www.example.com".
