⚠️ Important · Read Before Use ▾
  • This is an experimental prototype, not a secure email service.
  • Anyone can impersonate any sender – the “From” address is NOT verified.
  • Accounts expire at 00:00 UTC every day and must be recreated.
  • All messages disappear from the server after ~6 days (filebin retention).
  • Your private key is stored publicly (encrypted by password) – use a strong, unique password.
  • The public key registry is untrusted – no proof of ownership.
  • Do NOT share sensitive information.
